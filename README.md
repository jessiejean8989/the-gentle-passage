# Human Passages

**Digital companions for the major passages of life.**

Human Passages is a growing collection of thoughtful digital companions created to support people through meaningful life transitions.

The collection currently includes:

- **The Gentle Passage** — a gentle companion for families before, during and after a death.
- **The New Passage** — a companion for pregnancy, birth, welcoming home and becoming a family.
- **The Becoming Passage** — a companion for early teens and teens as they grow, change and become.

Each app is created with compassion, dignity, privacy, choice and practical support at its heart.

Created by Jessica Jean.

**Different passages. The same human heart.**
