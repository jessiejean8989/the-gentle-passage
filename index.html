Privacy Policy

The Gentle Passage is designed with privacy, dignity and care in mind.

Personal content you create within the app, including memories, photos, family details, wishes, tasks, dates and journal-style entries, is designed to be stored locally on your device.

The Gentle Passage does not require an account or login. Your personal content is intended to remain private on your device unless you choose to share it.

Photo access is requested only when you choose to add a photo within the app.

The Gentle Passage does not sell personal information or use personal information for advertising or tracking.

Locally stored information can be removed by deleting the relevant content where that option is available. Deleting the app will remove locally stored app data, subject to your device's own backup and restoration features.
